Lifecycle of the Application

From Lead to Installation

Create the Lead

Log into Stratus and create a lead, including all merchant-level information you have available.

Generate & Send

Select the agreement form matching the merchant's platform and pricing, generate it, and send or schedule it.

Merchant Signs

The merchant reviews, fills in any missing information, and signs the application.

Underwriting

Once submitted, the application goes to our Underwriting team for review.

Boarding & Maintenance

Once Underwriting completes their review, they send the account to Boarding and Maintenance to get it ready for install.

Schedule the Installation

You'll schedule the installation with the merchant and continue being a valuable asset to your client.

×

Finding the Account Status

Every lead in Stratus has an Account Status field near the top of the record, this is where you'll see where the application currently stands (for example, Approved/Boarded).
