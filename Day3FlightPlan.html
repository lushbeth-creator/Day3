From opportunity to relationship.

Day 3 follows the merchant journey. We start with creating enough quality conversations, then practice discovery and follow-up, move into the Decision Appointment and finish with what happens after the merchant says yes.

Build the opportunity

Prospect intentionally, use your network and create a clear reason for the first conversation.

Advance the conversation

Discover before you pitch, follow up with purpose and earn a Decision Appointment.

Protect the relationship

Set expectations, stay visible after installation and turn good merchant experiences into retention and referrals.
