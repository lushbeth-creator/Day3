You do not need to reinvent yourself.

Prospecting is not just finding strangers. Start by mapping the people, businesses and relationships already around you, then expand with intention.

Your Three Spheres

  • Friends & Family: highest trust, easiest conversations. Tell them what you're doing and ask who they know.
  • Businesses You Frequent: you're already their customer. Lead with the relationship and be curious first.
  • Previous Clients: trust is already built. A client who wasn't ready before may be now.

One pitfall to watch: your sphere gets you started; it doesn't build a career. Keep fueling your pipeline alongside it.

Your Prospecting Path
