Present Solutions

  • If you sell on price, you lose on price
  • How can you make sure our solutions benefit their business?
  • Tie every feature back to a specific pain point they've shared

Address Closing Concerns

  • Listen first, don't interrupt
  • Repeat the concern back in your own words
  • Respond with how our solution solves it

Ask For The Sale

  • If I am able to, will you...
  • Set the stage and timeline for the install process
  • Ask directly and confidently, don't be afraid of a pause

Collect Documentation

Minimum Information Requirements

DBA name, contact name/email, MCC code, equipment, and pricing.

Depending on your platform, there may be different fields to complete.
