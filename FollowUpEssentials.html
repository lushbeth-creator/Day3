“Just checking in” is not a strategy.

Most first conversations will not end with an appointment. That is where follow-up begins. Every touch should have a purpose: know who you are contacting, when you will reconnect, what you will bring and how you will ask for the next step.

Who

Know who you met

Leave a good impression on every employee; you never know who you are talking to. Follow up with the person who can move the conversation forward.

Log names, roles and notes in Stratus before you start the car.

When

Within 5 business days

The longer you wait, the colder the lead gets. Call first and email if you need to.

If the merchant gave you a timeline for making decisions, honor it exactly.

What

Bring a reason

Reference your last conversation, deliver what you promised or bring a relevant question. Use what you learned to show you were listening.

Follow-up should feel connected, never random.

How

Lock in the next touch

Get permission to come back before you leave, then show up exactly when you said you would. That is how trust starts.

Say it

“I'll be back in the area Thursday. I'll stop by then. Does that work?”

80%

Stay top of mind between visits.

Follow-up is not only calls and emails. When a merchant sees you in their world, you become familiar, and these habits show you are not just there for a sale.

Follow their social media

Like and comment on their posts. Build rapport there the same way you would at the counter.

Share what they share

Repost their specials, events and milestones on your own social media. Small businesses notice who supports them.

Show up as a customer

Stop in on a Saturday just to buy something. No pitch. Let them get used to seeing you in the area.

Know what they're promoting

Check their current specials or events and bring them up next time you talk.

Leave a genuine review

Once you've been a real customer, leave an honest review. It puts your name in front of the owner.

Bring something useful

A local event, a referral or an idea that helps their business. Value first, pitch later.
