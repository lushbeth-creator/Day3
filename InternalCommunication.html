After the "yes," the Senior Payments Advisor submits the application through Stratus. Then, our Underwriting and Boarding and Maintenance Teams work on getting your deal ready for installation.

Underwriting

The underwriting department is responsible for assessing the risk of all merchant applications while ensuring our standards for all applications and risks are followed. Our underwriting team utilizes different processes, procedures and standards to determine the risk associated with each account to make sound decisions that have suitable risk outcomes for Propelr.

Boarding & Maintenance

Our boarding team is responsible for setting up new merchant accounts on Propelr's supported platforms. Our Boarding Specialists ensure all card types, pricing, and equipment are correctly configured so the merchants can process payments in the most efficient manner.
